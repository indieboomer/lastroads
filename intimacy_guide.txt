Optional Intimacy and Character Keepsakes
Design intent
Add optional, discreet romantic and intimate encounters with selected adult NPCs. The tone should combine earthy humour, affection, temptation and the religious sensibilities of the game's alternative Renaissance setting.

Encounters are suggested through dialogue, restrained changes in character presentation and a cinematic fade to black. Sexual activity is never shown or described explicitly.

This is a secondary character interaction system. It must not become necessary for progression, an efficient way to obtain combat bonuses, or the default interaction with female characters.
Eligibility and consent
- Every participating NPC and player character must be unambiguously an adult.
- Enable encounters only for explicitly authored NPCs. Do not automatically apply the system to the entire cast.
- The Sister is excluded.
- A completed quest may unlock a more personal conversation. Intimacy is a separate mutual choice, never payment owed for completing the quest.
- Invitations require a suitable relationship, narrative context and private location.
- Exclude coercion, threats, captivity and incapacitating intoxication.
- The player can decline gracefully without losing quest rewards, services or progression.
- Include a final confirmation before the transition. Acceptance of flirtation alone is not acceptance of an intimate encounter.
NPCs should retain their own preferences and boundaries. Not every flirtation succeeds.
Encounter flow
1. A quest or relationship milestone unlocks an optional personal dialogue branch.
2. The NPC expresses interest in language consistent with their personality and the setting.
3. The player may reciprocate, keep the exchange playful, or decline.
4. If both agree, the conversation establishes a private place and an appropriate moment.
5. Show a brief, non-explicit visual change: a warmer expression, a removed hat or outer cloak, loosened hair, or a modest adjustment to clothing. Keep the character covered.
6. Fade to black before any sexual activity. Use a brief musical transition or ambient sound; no sexual sound effects.
7. Fade back into an ordinary aftermath: a quiet conversation, a shared drink, a knowing smile or a return to everyday business.
8. Unlock a character keepsake card after the first completed encounter.
Avoid prescribing a universal duration or time-of-day change. Any time advance must follow the world's existing simulation rules.
Religious and social tone
NPCs understand that such invitations may be considered improper or sinful. Express this through discretion, hesitation, humour or conflicting feelings rather than a repeated morality speech.

Different personalities respond differently: one jokes about confession, another values privacy, another openly acknowledges desire while remaining sincere in their faith. Christianity must remain part of their lived worldview.

Possible dialogue:
“Stay a little longer. I have better wine upstairs—and less respectable reasons for asking.”

“I ought to send you on your way. Heaven forgive me, I would rather you stayed.”

“Let the village keep its opinions. Still… use the side door.”

Possible player responses:
- “If you are certain, I would like to stay.”
- “A drink and your company. Nothing more tonight.”
- “You flatter me, but I should go.”
Do not make every religious character hypocritical or every intimate encounter an occasion for shame.
Same-sex encounters
Support authored same-sex invitations and encounters using the same consent rules, presentation quality and keepsake system.

Use approximately 10% of authored encounter opportunities as an initial content-planning target for specifically same-sex encounters. This is an editorial distribution, not a random probability rolled whenever the player speaks to an NPC.

Define each participating NPC's preferences consistently. Do not reroll attraction after loading a save, and do not infer it solely from the player's current appearance.

Social discretion may shape individual scenes, but same-sex intimacy must not automatically trigger ridicule, punishment, betrayal or a darker outcome.
Character keepsake cards
The card records a personal memory, inspired by the collectible structure of the first Witcher, with an original visual treatment.

Each card contains:
- An original painted portrait of the NPC.
- Their name and a short, character-specific title.
- A brief non-explicit recollection or quotation.
- The location of the encounter, where appropriate.
Portraits may show relaxed clothing, loose hair or a private smile, while remaining covered and consistent with the Old Master art direction. Do not use explicit imagery or copy existing Witcher artwork.

Store cards in the individual player's journal. Avoid conquest counters, rarity rankings, collection bonuses or achievements requiring intimacy. Repeated encounters must not produce duplicate cards or farmable rewards.
Co-op behaviour
An encounter belongs to the initiating player.
- Never pause the shared world or fade other players' screens.
- Other players must not be pulled into the encounter or receive its card.
- Use a short, safely staged private transition.
- Recheck safety before committing. If combat or another invalidating event occurs beforehand, postpone the encounter.
- Commit completion and the card unlock atomically to prevent duplication after disconnects or reloads.
- Keep intimate dialogue and journal details private to the participating player by default.
Implementation and acceptance
Implement the feature as data-driven dialogue branches with explicit adult eligibility, attraction preferences, relationship requirements, quest conditions, location checks and completion flags.

Provide a player setting to disable intimate invitations and their presentation without removing ordinary friendship dialogue or quest content.

The first implementation should contain one complete encounter, its decline path, its fade transition, its aftermath and one keepsake card. Validate this flow before expanding the roster.

Acceptance requires:
- Clear adult eligibility and mutual consent.
- Graceful refusal with no progression penalty.
- No explicit sexual visuals, descriptions or audio.
- Setting-appropriate dialogue and individual personality.
- Stable NPC preferences across saves.
- Correct per-player co-op state.
- No duplicate cards or repeatable mechanical advantage.
- No intimate content assigned to the Sister.
